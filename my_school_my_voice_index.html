Every student deserves to be heard.

A concept for a safe, respectful way for students to raise school-related concerns and share feedback.

Prototype only: This is a demonstration website. It does not send, save, or forward complaints. Do not enter real names, phone numbers, school details, or sensitive information here.

About the project

MY SCHOOL MY VOICE aims to help students communicate concerns about their school environment. Reports should be handled privately by authorised, trained adults and shared with the appropriate authority only when lawful and necessary.

How it should work

1. Share a concern

Students can describe a school-related issue through a protected process.

2. Safe review

An authorised safeguarding team reviews reports, protects privacy, and checks urgent safety concerns.

3. Appropriate action

Verified reports may be referred through official channels, with progress updates where appropriate.

Student report form — visual demo

This sample form is intentionally non-functional. A real service needs secure server-side storage, access controls, encryption, audit logs, a safeguarding process, retention rules, and review by a responsible organisation before accepting reports from children.
